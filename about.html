About Loerincz Engineering

Loerincz Engineering is an independent structural engineering practice specializing in structural analysis, structural design, and static analysis. With experience spanning over three decades, we have made significant contributions to numerous infrastructure projects, particularly in Germany.

  • Education & Qualifications

    Our practice is led by a Dipl. Ing. qualified civil engineer, schooled at a Gymnasium for Physics, Mathematics, and Mechanics before going on to higher education at the Technical University of Cluj-Napoca.
    ‍
    The diploma in civil engineering was earned with a thesis on the calculation and design of a prestressed concrete bridge over traffic routes — the foundation of a practice that has specialised in prestressed concrete and bridge structures ever since.

  • Professional Experience

    Our work began as a planning engineer at UVATERV A.G., developing software for static analysis programs. Over the years that has grown to span structural planning, construction supervision, and project management. This extensive experience includes high-profile projects such as:

    • Stuttgart 21 Project: Involved in tunnel and infrastructure structural planning, including the design of functional construction pits, diaphragm walls, and pile walls.
    • Bremerhaven, Lock and Steel Bridges: Managed the structural planning and construction supervision for these critical infrastructure projects.
    • Frankfurt Am Main, High-rise Building: Oversaw the static analysis and construction supervision for the redevelopment of a high-rise building.

  • Independent Practice

    Today we work as an independent structural planning and static engineering practice, focusing on engineering and high-rise buildings, steel construction, and prestressed concrete structures.

    Some of our notable projects are:

    • Railway Bridges and Retaining Structures for DB: Conducted static analysis and design for various railway bridge refurbishments and new constructions.
    • Industrial Pipeline and Steel bridge in Bavaria: Delivered comprehensive structural planning and static analysis for complex engineering structures.
